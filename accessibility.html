Accessibility Help

GLAMOUR+ West Africa is committed to making our site usable for as many people as possible, including readers who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology.

We're an independent, actively evolving publication, so this page reflects where we are today rather than a formal compliance certification.

If you run into anything on the site that's hard to use — text that's difficult to read, a control that doesn't work with a keyboard or screen reader, or a broken link — please tell us. Include the page you were on and, if possible, the device or browser you were using, and we'll look into it.
